BuildProof: verify a document

Drop a contract, supplementary agreement or acceptance act. Your browser computes its SHA-256 locally; only the hash is sent to the blockchain node. The file itself is never uploaded.

Drop a file here or click to choose
PDF or any other file. Nothing leaves your computer except its hash.

Sign as a party (no gas)

Open the attestation id you were sent, check that the document hash matches your copy, then sign with your wallet. You get a signature file; anyone (for example the other party) can submit it with buildproof relay.


  


  

    BuildProof is an open-source prototype (MIT). Demo documents are synthetic, with fictional parties.
